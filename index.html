Multi-Generational Tips

  • Pacing for Seniors: Limit continuous driving blocks to 2 hours maximum before a comfort/stretch stop. Ensure ground-floor or elevator accommodations.
  • Teen Engagement: Interactive boat tours, puffins in Elliston, and coastal photography keep teens energized and off screens.
  • Group Vehicles: With 8 people, splitting into 2 mid-sized SUVs provides comfortable seating across our family group.

Safety & Medical Note

Newfoundland has excellent regional medical clinics and hospitals in major hubs (Corner Brook, Gander, Clarenville, St. John's), but remote coastal areas can be isolated. Always travel with a stocked first-aid kit and senior prescription backups.

15-Day Family Itinerary (Optimized for 8 Travelers)

Balanced daily driving times and senior-friendly activity options.

2-Week Plan
Days 1 - 3 St. John's

Capital City Exploration

Settle in after your flight from Toronto. Explore the iconic Jellybean Row houses, visit Signal Hill for panoramic views (accessible by car for seniors), and watch the sunset at Cape Spear—the easternmost point in North America. Visit The Rooms museum for Newfoundland heritage, stroll along the Ocean Path, and enjoy fresh seafood at local restaurants with senior-friendly seating.

Day 4 Bonavista

Historic Coastline & Puffins

Drive along the scenic coast to Bonavista (approx 3.5 hours from St. John's). Visit the historic Cape Bonavista Lighthouse, one of Newfoundland's most photographed landmarks. Take a short drive to Elliston—the "Puffin Capital of North America"—for easy, flat walks suitable for seniors that offer excellent puffin viewing between May and August. Explore the charming harbour, seafood restaurants, and marine interpretation centre.

Day 5 Gander

Aviation Heritage

Continue to Gander (approx 3 hours from Bonavista). Known as the "Crossroads of the Atlantic," Gander played a pivotal role in WWII as a refueling stop for trans-Atlantic flights. Visit the Gander International Aviation Museum to learn about the city's aviation history and the 2001 "Plane Groundings" after 9/11. Explore Gander Lake and experience small-town Newfoundland hospitality.

Day 6 Lewisporte

Capital of the North

Travel to Lewisporte (approx 1.5-2 hours from Gander), known as the "Capital of the North." Explore the town's maritime heritage at the local museum, walk along Lewisporte Harbour, and venture into nearby outports of Notre Dame Bay. Enjoy scenic views of the bay, learn about the fishing industry, and experience authentic outport culture away from the main tourist paths.

Days 7 - 9 Twillingate

Iceberg Capital & Coastal Heritage

Journey to Twillingate (approx 2 hours from Lewisporte), the "Iceberg Capital of North America." From May to July, witness massive icebergs drifting along "Iceberg Alley" on boat tours or from shore. Visit the Twillingate Museum and Heritage Centre for maritime history, explore the Newfoundland Insectarium, take coastal boat tours for whale watching, and enjoy spectacular coastal outport scenery. The area offers both gentle shoreline walks and more challenging hikes for the active travelers.

Days 10 - 12 Gros Morne

Fjords, Tablelands & Coastline

Drive to Gros Morne National Park (approx 4.5 hours from Twillingate via Route 330 and 430), a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Take the famous Western Brook Pond boat tour through glacier-carved fjords (book in advance, gentle walk required). Explore the Mars-like Tablelands with interpretive guides (permits required for certain areas). hike the Green Gardens trail for coastal wilderness experiences. Enjoy coastal drives with whale-viewing opportunities, relax at Western Brook Pond interpretation centre, and savour locally sourced seafood dinners.

Days 13 - 15 St. John's

Farewell & Celebration

Begin the drive back to St. John's (approx 3.5-4 hours from Gros Morne via Route 1). Spend your final hours revisiting favorite spots or discovering new ones—the city is small enough to explore thoroughly. Celebrate the adventure with a farewell seafood dinner featuring the day's catch, explore any last-minute shops on Water Street, visit George Street for live music (teens!), and reflect on the 15-day island loop journey before departure the following morning.
